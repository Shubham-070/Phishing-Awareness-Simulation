This Was a Cybersecurity Awareness Simulation

The login page you just interacted with was a simulated phishing-awareness exercise. Its purpose is to demonstrate how attackers can create convincing login pages to trick users into entering sensitive information.

Cybersecurity awareness helps you recognize suspicious login pages, unexpected messages, misleading links, urgent requests, and other common social-engineering techniques before they become a security problem.

Stay Safe Online

  • Check the website address before signing in.
  • Never enter your password on an unfamiliar page.
  • Be cautious with urgent or unexpected messages.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication whenever possible.
